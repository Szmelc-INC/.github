# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

The Szmelc-INC organization follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). We welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Treat others with respect; harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and other harmful conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers of the affected repository through its documented contact route. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue or Discussion. If no private route is documented, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route without describing the incident. If the concern involves a maintainer or the organization, report abusive content through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Repository maintainers are responsible for applying this policy in their project spaces. They may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban an account. Actions should be proportionate and applied consistently. When it is safe and appropriate, maintainers should explain the decision.

This policy applies in organization spaces and when someone is publicly representing Szmelc-INC.
